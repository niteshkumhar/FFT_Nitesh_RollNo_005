    About Alliance School of Advanced Computing

    Vision

    To be recognized globally as an incubator for next-generation computing research, technological entrepreneurship, and hands-on software leadership.

    Mission

    • Provide curriculum aligned with top industry benchmarks across all undergraduate and postgraduate computing degrees.
    • Establish modern computational hubs for applied machine intelligence, security, and cloud frameworks.
    • Encourage collaborative research and startup culture among students and faculty members.

    Core Objectives

    • Equip students across B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc, M.Tech, MCA, and M.Sc with deep problem-solving and algorithmic foundations.
    • Maintain 100% experiential learning through real-time capstone projects, coding bootcamps, and industrial internships.
